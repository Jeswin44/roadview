The Challenge

Problem Statement & Real-World Motivation

The Urban Hazard Epidemic

  • Rapid infrastructure degradation creates acute craters, waterlogged sinkholes, and jagged trenches.
  • Thousands of accidents and vehicle breakdowns happen daily due to unmapped road obstacles.
  • Standard GPS apps (Google Maps, Waze) alert for traffic slowdowns, but never warn about underbody road obstacles.

The Vehicle Clearance Gap

  • Sedans & Hatchbacks (160–165mm): Suffer oil sump fractures, bumper shearing, and subframe misalignment.
  • Two-Wheelers: Face sudden front-wheel entrapment and catastrophic rider rollover when depth exceeds 8–10 cm.
  • SUVs: Pass safely over hazards that would total a standard low-slung passenger vehicle.

The Municipal Reporting Bottleneck

Citizens rarely report road damage due to tedious manual bureaucracy. As a result, municipal agencies (NHAI, BBMP, PWD) remain unaware of hazardous sinkholes until major accidents occur.

The Innovation

Proposed Solution: RoadGuard AI

1. Road Vision Scanner

Simulates computer vision scanning from driver dashcams, estimating pothole depth, diameter, and classification.

2. Passability Physics

Calculates dynamic suspension compression, speed factors, and ground clearance margins for specific cars & bikes.

3. Google Maps Radar

Integrates browser GPS to detect and visualize the 6 closest road segments with instant color-coded safety pins.

4. Municipal Dispatch

Automatically triggers SLA repair tickets (24h Emergency / 48h Priority) directly to road authorities with official tracking IDs.

Formulations

Mathematical & Physical Modeling

1. Haversine Great-Circle Distance

Used to find the closest roads from GPS coordinate (φ1, λ1) to road center (φ2, λ2):

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1)·cos(φ2)·sin²(Δλ/2)
c = 2 · atan2(√a, √(1-a))
Distance = R · c (R = 6,371 km)

2. Dynamic Suspension Sag

Accounts for vehicle weight transfer and approach velocity:

Sag = min(85mm, (Depth × 0.35) × (Weight/1500) × K_stiff × F_speed)
Effective Clearance = GC - Depth - (Sag × 0.5)

3. Pothole Severity Index (PSI)

PSI = min(99, (Depth_cm × 3.8) + (Diameter_cm × 0.35) + WaterLoggedBonus)

If PSI ≥ 70%, the hazard is automatically escalated to an emergency municipal dispatch ticket.

Implementation Details

Core Java Modules & Design Patterns

VehiclePassabilityService

Physics Engine: Evaluates ground clearance, calculates rim bend probability, shock absorber wear, and estimated repair cost in ₹ INR.

RoadSafetyService

Spatial Engine: Catalogs 12 road segments across Bengaluru, executes the Haversine algorithm, and delivers the 6 nearest road corridors.

PotholeDetectionService

Hazard Repository: Ingests road anomalies, classifies depth & diameter, and triggers automated municipal escalation.

RoadGuardServer

Embedded Microservice: Uses HttpServer to route requests for vehicles, roads, passability evaluations, and dispatches.

AuthorityReportingService

Civic Dispatch: Formats official tickets for NHAI, BBMP, and PWD with auto-generated SLA resolution deadlines.

Domain Models

Vehicle, RoadSegment, Pothole, PassabilityResult, and DispatchReport encapsulate OOP entities.

Civic Technology

Autonomous Municipal Dispatching

Automated Ticket Routing

  • National Highways (NHAI): Outer Ring Road & Expressways.
  • Municipal Corporations (BBMP): Major arterial zones & ward streets.
  • State Public Works (PWD): State highways & bypass corridors.

Enforced SLA Resolution Times

  • Emergency (24 Hours): Depth > 10 cm, critical rollover risk.
  • Priority (48 Hours): Medium depth craters, edge breaks.
  • Routine (7 Days): Minor surface spalls and rumble ripples.

Live Citizen Transparency Console

Commuters can view public dispatch tickets with tracking codes (e.g. NHAI-KA-BLR-2026-9481), providing complete transparency into civic repair workflows.
